# Prompts to paste into Copilot (version 3)

Owner: Tomas. Review date: 2026-11-21. Rollback: `rm rsvp/comps/PASTE-PROMPTS.md`.

Version 3, 2026-11-14. Every prompt uses the one canonical style lock in `DESIGN-SPEC.md` section 6 (`assets/STYLE.md` has the same words in prefix form). It keeps the opening that made the face's soft art, "Loose botanical watercolour of ...", lets the washes lead, and allows only a few broken ink lines, never a closed outline: the pen-outlined sketchbook look of the first four mushrooms is retired (user, 2026-11-14). Mushrooms stay at two, the fly agaric and the Caesar's pair; the `porcini`, parasol and `inkcaps` are dropped.

## How to use

1. Open `Copilot`, choose Think Deeper, start a new chat for each prompt.
2. Attach `comps/art/v16/olive.webp` (the olive branch, sent with this file) with the `+`, as the look to match. Do not attach mushroom pictures.
3. Copy one block, paste it, send, then download the full-size picture. One picture per prompt, in this order. Blocks 1 and 2 already exist as kept pictures in the older pen style; run them only if you want them redone in this look.
4. If one has a dark outline all round, hatching, soil crumbs, shine or a stiff catalogue pose, send the same prompt once more. Judge with the checklist in `DESIGN-SPEC.md` section 12.
5. Tell me when you have a few. I take the newest files from Downloads in this order, check each, tone it to the page and place it.

## 1. M5: `mush-fly-agaric-pair.webp`

```text
Loose botanical watercolour of two fly agarics, Amanita muscaria, a rounded dusty terracotta-red cap with white flecks left as bare paper and a smaller one half open, pale gills in a few ink strokes, white stems with a ragged skirt and a bulbous base, a few dots of earth at the base. Warm cotton paper, soft muted Mediterranean palette of olive, sage, straw, dusty terracotta and faded lemon, restrained saturation. Translucent layered washes with soft wet edges, colour that fades out rather than stopping at a line, white of the paper for highlights, light from the upper left. Only a few thin broken dark-brown ink lines, in places, never a closed outline around a shape. Few details: no hatching, no stippling, no texture strokes, no soil or crumbs, no gloss, no 3D modelling; a natural slightly asymmetric pose, not a catalogue plate. One element, isolated with wide margins. Plain flat off-white background, no ground, no shadow, no people, no text, no frame. Square 1:1, high resolution.
```
