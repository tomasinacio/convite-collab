# Five prompts for Copilot

Owner: Tomas. Review date: 2026-11-21. Rollback: `rm rsvp/comps/PASTE-PROMPTS-COPILOT.md`.

These use the exact template that made the face's wheat, lavender and olive (`comps/V12-CHOREOGRAPHY-ADVICE.md` section 5). Think Deeper, new chat each, download full size.

Already made through `Higgsfield` and waiting in `assets/inbox/` (suffix `hf3-v12`): peppers, fly agarics, Caesar's mushrooms, bee, fennel. No need to run those.

## 1. Wild carrot

Why: a white flower, so the grey background lets it be cut out.

```text
Loose botanical watercolour of three stems of wild carrot, Daucus carota, lacy flat white umbels, one with a tiny dark purple centre floret, one curled into a nest as it goes to seed, fine green feathery leaves. Warm cotton paper, thin uneven dark-brown ink lines, soft muted Mediterranean palette, visible paper grain, soft wash edges, white of the paper for highlights, light from the upper left. One element, isolated and centred. Plain flat even light warm-grey background, no gradient, no shadow, no text, no frame. Portrait 2:3, high resolution.
```
