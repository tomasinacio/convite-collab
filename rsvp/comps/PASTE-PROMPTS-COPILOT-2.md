# Five more prompts for Copilot

Owner: Tomas. Review date: 2026-11-21. Rollback: `rm rsvp/comps/PASTE-PROMPTS-COPILOT-2.md`.

Same exact template as the last five, which gave the best results. These redo the `Higgsfield` pieces in Copilot's hand so the whole set matches. Run the first four; the fifth is optional.

## 1. Hot peppers

Why: you asked for these; the `Higgsfield` version has a different hand.

```text
Loose botanical watercolour of a bunch of five long thin dried red hot peppers hanging from a loop of rough twine, tied at their stalks. Warm cotton paper, thin uneven dark-brown ink lines, soft muted Mediterranean palette, visible paper grain, soft wash edges, white of the paper for highlights, light from the upper left. One element, isolated and centred. Plain flat off-white background, no shadow, no text, no frame. Portrait 2:3, high resolution.
```
