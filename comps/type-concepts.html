Four ways to write Maya e Tomás

The same card four times: same art, same colours, same layout, same placeholder text. Only the lettering changes, so you can compare fairly. Under each card is a test of the accented letters. Opening line, date and place are still TODO(user).

Show every card in

1Light handwriting

Allura with EB Garamond (DESIGN.md option B)

Relaxed, light handwriting with a classic book typeface: warm and countryside, still easy to read.

Recommended

2No handwriting

EB Garamond italic with EB Garamond (new option)

The names in the italic of the same classic book typeface: the calmest and easiest to read, like a finely printed card.

3Bold handwriting

Great Vibes with Lora (DESIGN.md option A)

Rounder, heavier handwriting: the clearest script on a small screen, but the one most often seen on wedding templates.

4Formal calligraphy

Pinyon Script with Cormorant Garamond (DESIGN.md option C)

Formal pen-and-ink calligraphy: the most ceremonial, more city than countryside; its thin strokes get faint on small phones.

Watercolours: USDA Pomological Watercolor Collection, public domain (see assets/LOG.md). Fonts: Google Fonts, all with the Portuguese and Spanish accents.
