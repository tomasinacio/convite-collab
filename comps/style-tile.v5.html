The invitation face

Your deck, made vertical. A tall sheet of handmade paper, folded once and closed with a wax seal and twine. Tap the seal: the top lifts up and the paper grows downward. A full pastoral scene in line and wash then fills it from top to bottom: sky and swallows, hills, the names with a drawn olive sprig, a villa by the water, an olive tree, flowers. The guest scrolls down to read on. The art is cut from your own deck as a stand-in. Real artwork comes in stage 3, once you approve the look.

Closed · try it

Tap it: the seal and twine fall away, the top swings up on the fold, the paper grows downward, then the scene draws in, line first and colour second. Nothing moves sideways.

ii

What this tile decides

  1. Vertical opening only.Your pick: one tall sheet with one fold across it. The top part is folded down and sealed; on a tap it lifts up on the fold while the paper grows downward. The guest then scrolls down.
  2. Paint in the paper.The art has no drop shadows; it is multiplied into the paper like pigment. Only the sheet and the wax cast soft shadows. One drawing language: line and wash. The few pure ink lines draw stroke by stroke.
  3. Names lead.Maya e Tomás sit on bare paper just below the fold, in olive script. The scene surrounds them and never sits behind words.
  4. Full, but not contrasty.The paper still carries most of the face. Terracotta is in the wax and the flowers; blue-grey is in the clouds and swallows.
  5. Soft paper grain.Fine cotton tooth and gently torn edges. The visible handmade grain is in grain-compare.html; your pick.
  6. Type, your pick.Allura for the names only; EB Garamond for every word a guest must read, at 16 px or more.
